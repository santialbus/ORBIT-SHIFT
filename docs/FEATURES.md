# Features
**Gameplay** 3 orbits · automatic orbital motion · 2 control modes · 4 obstacle kinds + combined rows + 6 patterns · fairness guarantee · crystals · combo x1–x8 ·
perfect shifts · 5 difficulty phases from JSON · pooling · death FX, camera shake, vibration · pause/resume with countdown · app-switch auto-pause.
**UI** splash · menu (PLAY, best, crystals, collection, missions, settings, progress) · HUD (score, combo + progress bar, crystals, phase bar, pause) · pause menu ·
results (count-up, record animation + confetti, stats, rewards, PLAY AGAIN/MENU) · collection (3D preview, buy/equip, locked items, cost) · missions & achievements ·
settings (music, SFX, volumes, vibration, controls, graphics, tutorial toggle/replay, reset with confirmation, version) · interactive 5-step tutorial.
**Progression** Energy Crystals · 20 sphere skins (8 shader styles) · 5 core variants (scenery only) · 3 daily missions (local date) · 4 endless objectives ·
14 achievements · statistics (runs, record, total time, best combo/time, crystals, dodges, perfect shifts, records broken, skins unlocked).
**Persistence** atomic save, backup, checksum, validation, migration, autosave, save on pause/quit.
**Audio** 15 SFX + menu/game music, all synthesized by `tools/generate_audio.py` (original, no licensing issues).
**Visual** neon sci-fi minimal: faceted core with energy veins, glowing orbits, emissive obstacles/crystals, halo + trail, bloom, starfield, animated floor.
**Not included (by design for the beta)** ads, IAP, analytics, cloud save, leaderboards — see ROADMAP.
